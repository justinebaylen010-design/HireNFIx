    System overview and description

    HireNFix: AI-integrated service booking for Valenzuela City

    HireNFix is a mobile application that connects residents with local service providers such as plumbers, electricians, carpenters and painters. It has two parts that share the same data:

    • The mobile app for Residents and Local Service Providers. Both can use one account in either mode.
    • The Admin Web System, opened in a desktop browser, where Administrators verify users, review reports and maintain the platform.

    An AI help assistant named Fixie answers questions inside the app, and AI also helps generate the questions in the provider skills test.

    Background of HireNFix

    Why HireNFix was built

    Home repairs and small jobs often depend on word of mouth, with no easy way to check who is trustworthy or to keep a record of the work.

    Hard to find the right person

    Residents need a simple place to find nearby providers by trade, rating and experience.

    Trust needs proof

    HireNFix verifies residents with an ID and proof of billing, and providers with a Barangay Clearance and two assessments.

    Bookings need a record

    Every deal gets a contract, proof of service and a digital receipt, so both sides can refer back to what was agreed.

    System purpose and objectives

    What the system is meant to do

    • Let residents discover and book local service providers efficiently.
    • Verify residents and providers before they transact.
    • Help residents judge providers through skill-level tags, ratings, portfolios and reviews.
    • Support communication through in-app messaging and notifications.
    • Manage provider workload fairly with the transaction token system.
    • Record agreements, proof of service and receipts for each deal.
    • Give Administrators tools to handle reports, disputes, assessments and legal documents.
    • Offer a help assistant that guides users through the app.
    Target users

    Three roles, one platform

    Resident

    A Valenzuela City resident who needs a service done. Residents register with a valid ID and proof of billing, post requests, book providers, pay, rate and report.

    Resident guide

    Local Service Provider

    A local worker who offers a trade. Providers submit a Barangay Clearance, take the SJT and WIA, publish offers, apply to jobs and manage tokens.

    Provider guide

    Administrator

    Authorized staff who use the Admin Web System to verify users, review reports, maintain assessments, legal text and chatbot answers.

    How HireNFix works

    The booking lifecycle

    Two routes lead to the same result: a confirmed deal, completed work and a review.

    Route 1: Job request

    • Resident posts a request
    • Providers apply
    • Resident accepts one
    • Deal confirmed

    Route 2: Direct booking

    • Provider lists a service
    • Resident taps Book Now
    • Provider accepts in 24 hours
    • Deal confirmed

    After the deal is confirmed

    • Schedule and chat
    • Work done
    • Provider uploads proof
    • Resident confirms (or 48-hour auto-complete)
    • Digital receipt
    • Review or report

    Supported devices and operating systems

    Devices, internet and storage

    Mobile devices

    Android phones and tablets running Android 8.0 or later with at least 2 GB of RAM. Support for other mobile platforms is not stated in the manuals.

    Administrator computers

    Windows 10 or 11, macOS 11, or a modern Linux distribution, using the latest Chrome, Firefox, Edge or Safari with JavaScript enabled.

    Internet and storage

    A stable internet connection is needed to install and use the app. The app needs at least 300 MB of free storage. Admins need 1 GB free (5 GB recommended).
