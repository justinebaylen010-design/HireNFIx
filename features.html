    Where the features fit together

    The same capabilities, grouped by what they help people do.

    Account management

    Registration, ID and billing verification, Barangay Clearance, login and password recovery, role switching, and Administrator approval.

    Service booking and management

    Job requests, applications, direct booking, service offers, contracts, a calendar and proof of service.

    Messaging and notifications

    Chats between Resident and Provider after acceptance, plus alerts for applications, bookings and restored tokens.

    Payment and transaction management

    Agreed amounts in the contract, a digital receipt per deal and an Activity Ledger for Administrators.

    Assessment and verification

    SJT skill-level tags, the internal WIA, and Administrator review of documents and flagged accounts.

    Feedback, reviews and disputes

    Star ratings, written reviews, report types, token freezes, suspensions and an Account Health gauge.
