    Before installing the application

    Have these ready so setup takes only a few minutes.

    • An Android phone running Android 8.0 or later
    • At least 2 GB of RAM and 300 MB of free storage
    • A stable internet connection
    • An email address you can open
    • Residents: a valid government ID and a recent proof of billing (for example Meralco, Maynilad or internet bill)
    • Providers: the same documents, plus your Barangay Clearance
    Take clear photos of your documents in good light before you start. You upload them during registration.
    2

    Check device compatibility

    HireNFix needs the following minimum specifications.

    RequirementMinimum
    Mobile OSAndroid 8.0
    RAMAt least 2 GB
    StorageAt least 300 MB available
    InternetStable connection
    To see your Android version, open your phone’s Settings and look for About phone. Menu names can differ slightly between phone brands.
    3

    Download the application installer

    1. On your phone, scan the official HireNFix QR code with the camera or a QR scanner app.
    2. Or tap the button below to open the access link.
    3. Your phone opens the HireNFix download page.
    Open download linkDownload page
    Only use the QR code and link published on this website or in the official HireNFix manuals.
    4

    Install the app

    After you open the link or scan the QR code, install HireNFix on your Android phone.

    1. Select Install when your phone offers it.
    2. If Android asks you to confirm, follow the prompts on your screen.
    3. Wait for the installation to finish, then continue to step 5.
    Keep a stable internet connection until the installation finishes.
    5

    Open the HireNFix application

    1. Find the HireNFix icon on your home screen or in your app list.
    2. Tap it to launch the app.
    3. The Login screen opens. If you are new, tap Don’t have an account? Register.
    6

    Account registration

    1. Enter a Username and select your Barangay.
    2. Enter your Email Address, then a Password and Confirm Password.
    3. Tick the boxes for the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.
    4. Tap Register as Resident.

    Full registration steps · Register as a provider

    7

    Email verification

    1. After you register, the app sends a verification email to the address you used.
    2. Open the email and select the verification link.
    3. The link takes you back to the HireNFix app automatically.
    4. Finish your account information in the app (step 8).
    5. If nothing arrives, check your spam folder, then use Resend Confirmation Email on the Login screen.
    8

    First-time account setup

    Verification protects everyone on the platform.

    1. Select the Type of Valid ID and upload a clear photo. Tap Continue.
    2. Select the Type of Billing Statement and upload a photo of it.
    3. Tick Register as Worker? only if you also want to offer services.
    4. Tap Complete Registration. Your account is now pending Administrator review.
    Until you are verified you are a Basic User with browse-only access. Tap Verify Now on your dashboard or profile any time.
    9

    Log in to the application

    1. Open HireNFix.
    2. Enter your registered email address or username.
    3. Enter your password and tap Login.
    4. Pending accounts enter as Basic Users. Approved accounts enter as Verified Residents.

    Forgot your password? Tap Forgot Password? and follow these steps.
