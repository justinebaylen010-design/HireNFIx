What is HireNFix?

One app for booking trusted local help

HireNFix is a mobile application for Valenzuela City. Residents post a job or book a listed service. Local service providers apply, accept bookings and build a public profile with ratings, a skill-level tag and a portfolio. Administrators verify accounts and keep the platform safe from a web dashboard.

Purpose of the application

  • Make it quick for residents to find service providers near them.
  • Give local workers a clear way to be found, booked and reviewed.
  • Keep every booking on record with contracts, digital receipts and reports.

How does HireNFix work?

From sign-up to review in five steps

The same flow applies whether you post a job request or book a service a provider has already listed.

  1. Create an account

    Register with your details, valid ID and proof of billing. An Administrator verifies you.

  2. Find local providers

    Browse service categories and provider profiles in the Hire Hub.

  3. Book or request a service

    Tap Book Now on a listing, or post a job and let providers apply.

  4. Complete the transaction

    Agree on the schedule, get the work done, and receive a digital receipt.

  5. Submit feedback

    Rate and review the provider, or report a problem to the Administrator.

Why choose HireNFix?

Trust and a clear record on every job

Built for Valenzuela City, with checks before work starts and records after it ends.

Verified providers

Providers submit a Barangay Clearance, take a skills test and an integrity assessment, and an Administrator reviews them before they can work.

Every deal on record

Agreed amounts go into a service contract, and a digital receipt is created when the job is completed.

Talk inside the app

Chat with the other party once a booking is accepted, and get an alert for every status change.

Ratings you can check

Residents rate completed jobs from 1 to 5 stars. Ratings and reports feed each provider's Account Health.
