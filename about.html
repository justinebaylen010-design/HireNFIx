    About HireNFix

    HireNFix is an AI-integrated mobile application for efficient local service provider booking in Valenzuela City. It helps residents find and book verified local workers, and helps those workers get found, booked and reviewed.

    A companion Admin Web System lets authorized staff verify accounts, resolve reports and keep the platform running smoothly.

    Project background

    The application and the study

    HireNFix is an AI-integrated mobile application that was developed with a purpose of efficiently facilitating interactions between residents and local service providers, concerning residential services provision. The application is comprised of relevant features that allows the residents to conveniently source and request available household services in their area, as well as enables the service providers to utilize the platform to easily engage with potential residential clients and showcase their reliability and capabilities as a worker.

    The study employed a descriptive-developmental research design, along with a Sequential Exploratory Mixed-Methods approach to obtain substantial qualitative and quantitative insights. The system was developed following the Agile Lean Software Development approach to optimize efficiency, minimize wastes, and support incremental system improvement across development iterations.

    The proponents employed a four-point Likert scale survey questionnaire to enable stakeholders to evaluate the system’s features and determine whether it met the software quality standards defined on ISO/IEC 25010. The overall evaluation yielded a “Strongly Agree” interpretation from 79 respondents, classified into four stakeholder groups: 50 residents and 25 service providers of Brgy. Rincon, Valenzuela City; 1 barangay personnel from the aforementioned locale; and 3 IT experts. Collectively, the system obtained positive feedback and a high degree of acceptance among its end-users, suggesting the system’s reliability as an effective platform for streamlining the interactions between residents and local service providers involving residential service provision and procurement.

    The proposed system

    To resolve the existing setbacks among residents and service providers, the proponents proposed the development of an AI-integrated mobile application called “HireNFix,” that aspires to streamline the process of linking residents to nearby service providers around Valenzuela City. The system presents functionalities that allow users to efficiently and conveniently navigate and book a local service provider that corresponds to their needs and premises. This includes a system module that ensures the availability and reliability of service providers, communication features to remain in touch with the booked provider, and a rate and review function for the residents to leave feedback regarding the performance of the booked service provider.

    Local service providers undergo a verification process in the application to ensure their credibility, and are given the privilege to accept, refuse, or cancel a service request from a residential client. Payment transactions are monitored and recorded by the system for transparency purposes, and an AI chat assistant assists users in finding a suitable service provider according to their need and in navigating the application.

    The main objective of HireNFix is to serve as a centralized platform and a digital bridge where residents can connect with local service providers around Valenzuela City with optimal efficiency. Through this platform, residents can assess the availability, trustworthiness, and credibility of the local service providers in the system. The application also gives local service providers an avenue to showcase their skills and reliability, establish their credibility, boost their visibility, and earn job opportunities rather than relying on scattered channels like community groups on social media.

    Mission and objectives

    Mission

    To make it simple and safe for Valenzuela City residents to find, book and review trusted local service providers, and to give local workers a fair way to be discovered.

    • Connect residents with verified local service providers.
    • Build trust with ID checks, a Barangay Clearance, skill and integrity assessments, ratings and reviews.
    • Record every deal with a contract, proof of service and a digital receipt.
    • Give Administrators clear tools to review reports and moderate fairly.
    • Help users with an in-app AI assistant.

    Terms and Conditions

    Accounts and verification

    • You must accept the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy when you register.
    • Residents upload a valid ID and proof of billing. Providers also upload a Barangay Clearance and complete two assessments.
    • Features such as requests, booking and messaging are restricted until an Administrator verifies you.

    Bookings and payment

    • Providers have up to 24 hours to accept or decline a direct booking.
    • Payment is cash. The agreed amount is written into a service contract with signature fields for the Resident, the Worker and a Witness.
    • Cancellation is not available once the service day begins at 12:00 AM.
    • A provider must upload proof of service to mark a job complete. If the Resident does not confirm within 48 hours, the job is marked Completed automatically.

    Reviews, reports and enforcement

    • Ratings, reviews and reports can only be submitted after a job is Completed.
    • Administrators may freeze tokens, suspend or ban accounts after reviewing a dispute.

    Privacy Policy

    Information the app handles

    • Account details: username, barangay, email address and contact details.
    • Verification documents: a valid ID, proof of billing, and for providers a Barangay Clearance and portfolio images.
    • Service activity: requests, bookings, contracts, receipts, messages, reviews and reports.
    • Location: your current location can be used to show or notify nearby providers.

    Who can see it

    • Administrators review verification documents and handle reports.
    • Residents see a provider’s public profile: name, trade, rating, skill-level tag, portfolio and reviews.
    • Work Integrity Assessment results are internal to Administrators and never shown to Residents.

    Security

    • Administrator actions and exports are recorded in an audit trail.
    • Raw database credentials are never exposed in the Admin browser.

    User responsibilities

    Residents

    • Give accurate details and genuine documents.
    • Provide the correct address and be available at the agreed time.
    • Write honest reviews and report real issues with facts.

    Providers

    • Keep your profile, services and portfolio truthful.
    • Answer bookings within 24 hours and honor accepted jobs.
    • Upload real proof of service and complete assessments honestly.

    Administrators

    • Verify accounts fairly and keep credentials private.
    • Apply suspensions or bans only with proper justification.
    • Publish legal updates carefully, keeping contract placeholders intact.
